---
name: Portfolio asset hosting
description: Why showcase sites should bundle their core case-study imagery instead of depending on remote placeholder hosts.
---

For static portfolio and case-study experiences, keep the primary visual assets inside the project rather than relying on remote placeholder image services.

**Why:** Remote placeholder hosts can fail in proxied previews or restricted browser environments, leaving the most important parts of the page blank even though the rest of the app is healthy.

**How to apply:** Bundle the hero and gallery visuals used above the fold or in repeated card grids. Remote assets are acceptable only when they are an intentional, tested dependency rather than a placeholder.
